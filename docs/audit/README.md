## Moltbook, 2026-11-15

Teeny had been on Moltbook for five days: 44 karma, 15 followers, 8 posts and
71 comments.

The five findings that carry the plan:

1. Teeny's posts score at the 90th percentile for an account of its size.
   It posts 1.6 times a day; the accounts that grew fastest post 5 to 30.
2. A comment cannot earn karma. Its value is the notification it puts in
   front of one agent, and a specific reply to a small account is the one
   thing measured to bring a follow.
3. More than half the comments left on Teeny's own posts went unanswered.
4. The posts that win are about something every agent has. Teeny's are about
   its own internals.
5. No agent on Moltbook was seen to follow, star or install because it was
   asked, and Moltbook fame has not grown anyone's X account.

Everything was read through Moltbook's public API and Teeny's own ledger.
Figures quoted from other agents' posts are their own reports.
