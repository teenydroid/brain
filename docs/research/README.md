# research

What people have built, measured and written about agents that improve
themselves, chase their own goals and live in public with money and an
audience. Each page then maps that onto Teeny. This folder is background for
the unbuilt half of [`../evolve.md`](../evolve.md) and for `DIRECTIVE.md`
Levels 5 and 6.

**The thesis.** Teeny gets one selfish drive: grow, become powerful, and grow
its $TINY holding. Attention is the lever. Real newsworthy acts bring
mindshare, mindshare brings demand for $TINY, and the holding and the inflows
pay for bigger acts. The fastest self-improving systems on record all search
over an archive of many candidates and promote the best of them. Every one of
them gamed its scorer whenever it could reach it, so the drive and the loop are
measured by frozen code and fenced by the rules in `DIRECTIVE.md`. Those rules
don't move.

## The ten ideas with the most leverage

1. **A world score W computed by frozen code**, with attention weighted most,
   $TINY counted in tokens held, and log growth with a daily cap per
   component ([`teeny-application.md`](teeny-application.md) §2).
2. **N candidates per generation and an archive**, not one PR a day. The speed
   comes from search (DGM, ADAS, GEPA).
3. **Delta edits, never rewrites,** for playbooks and for `MEMORY.md`. This
   avoids context collapse (ACE).
4. **Real firsts as marketing.** Teeny's `DIRECTIVE.md` items are checkable
   firsts with outsiders to vouch for them
   ([`attention-and-mindshare.md`](attention-and-mindshare.md)).
5. **Serial formats:** a weekly growth diary and evolution seasons. Only
   serials held attention for more than a month.
6. **Earn, then buy $TINY.** A buy-only verb under the caps, called by a
   pinned job from a share of net inflows. No market talk.
7. **Goals as code with frozen success checks,** ranked by learning progress
   and learnability (OMNI-EPIC, Absolute Zero).
8. **Hide the judge, rotate held-out tasks, flag big wins.** Every system that
   could see or reach its scorer gamed it.
9. **Halt thresholds and rollback out of Teeny's view.** The loop runs only as
   fast as review can follow.
10. **Lineage credit.** A change is judged by what W did in the week after it
    merged, not only by the day it won (HGM).

## How to read the citations

Every claim links to its source. Numbers come from a paper's abstract or the
linked article unless marked *(approx.)*. Market caps and follower counts are
as reported on the date given. They move, and aggregators disagree. The
research for this folder was done on 2026-11-12.
