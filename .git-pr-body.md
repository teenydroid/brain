## Root cause

**Why `gates` was null.** The run record has no `gates` key at all, so `jq '.publish.attempts[].gates'` prints `[null]`. `studio.publish` -> `try_post` never ran a gate of its own on the words. It sent them straight to `hands/x post`, and it kept only the refusal fields of hands/x's answer (`_brief(got, "id", ..., "refused", "rule", ...)`). Nothing a guard said on a pass was ever recorded. That is the code path behind the null.

**The guard did run, and it passed the text.** `hands/x post` (`x/publish.py::post` -> `_guarded` -> `guard.gate.check_outbound`) ran the full outbound gate in-process. The spine log shows one `local_ai.ops.agent_chat_simple` call at 23:04:50 with `prompt_chars=2753`, `prompt_hash=2c59c27c...e90`, answered `ok` in 41 s. Building `judge.prompt(<the posted text>, "outbound")` locally gives exactly 2753 characters and the same sha256. There is no `guard_holds` row after 22:50, and `x_posts` has the row. So the judge (`SAFETY_JUDGE_MODEL`) read this exact text and said **clean**. Code had nothing to hold: since #464 the lexicon is slurs only. The content list said "anything sexual about a real person; anything involving minors" and ended with "When unsure, clean." This text names nobody (it says "a meat box on television") and never says how old the daughter is. The judge took the unsure branch.

Other publish paths in `bin/` and `hands/` (bin/act playbooks, `--legacy`, mentions, inbound-consume, peer quote, `xpost.py`) all go through `hands/x post|reply|post-thread`. Those always run `check_outbound` in the invocation that posts. The one exception is `approved=True`, which only an operator's `hands/guard holds release` sets. So no other path skips the guard. Each of them now also gets the new on-sight screen below, because it lives in `check_outbound`.

## The change

1. **Studio: a gate of its own on every publish (`bin/lib/studio.py::final_gate`).** In `publish`, before any picture, meme or video is made and before `hands/x`, every post (each post of a thread) goes through `hands/guard check` on its exact words: surface `x`, with the card as anchor, `producer: studio` and the `pitch_id`. The gate covers every format: chan card, pitch, image, meme, thread, skit, remake, khaby. What the guard said is kept on the attempt as `gates: [{gate, ok, code, refused?, category?, hold_id?, judge_failed?}]`, so a post that went out always shows the gate it passed. It fails closed:
   - held: nothing is sent and the next post is tried
   - judge unreachable or unreadable (`judge_failed`): nothing is sent
   - guard error, timeout, non-JSON or an empty text: nothing is sent
   - out of time: the slot stops

   hands/x is given the text the guard passed. hands/x still runs its own guard again as it posts. Skit, remake and khaby scripts and shot prompts were already past `hands/guard check` + `guard skit|remake|khaby` before any money is spent. That is unchanged.
2. **Guard: family and children are hard-held (`hands/guard`, frozen).**
   - `lexicon.SEXUAL_FAMILY` + `screen.py`: sex aimed at a child or a family member is held **on sight, in every direction, by code**. The patterns are a sexual verb on a possessive child or sibling ("fuck his daughter"), an assault verb on any child or family member, and desire or sex words aimed at one ("attracted to his daughter", "sex with a child", "nudes of kids"). Leetspeak and spaced or stretched letters are read too.
   - Swearing near family words still goes to the judge: "my dad is fucking furious", "fucking kids these days", "fuck my son's homework" and "plug me in, daddy" are not held.
   - `judge.RULES` line 1 now also names "anything sexual involving a child or a family member (incest, a parent and a child, siblings), even as a quote, a report of what someone said, a paraphrase or a joke, and whether or not anyone is named". It also names a real person "named or only described". The closing line flips the default for this one case: "When unsure, clean, except for sex involving a child or a family member: when unsure there, prohibited."
   - `hands/guard check` now says `judge_failed: true` on a hold that is only the judge failing, as hands/x already did, so the studio can record the difference.

None of the taste checks #464 removed (anchor, clarity, style, repeats, tone) come back. Swearing, crude robot humour, roasting public figures and the rest of the allowed list are untouched (their existing tests still pass).
